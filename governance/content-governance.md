# Content governance

## Publish

Publish content that:

* answers a real buyer, practitioner, search or AI-retrieval need;
* explains an approved MagicWorks service, framework, boundary or method;
* is useful without requiring confidential context;
* can be maintained over time;
* has a clear canonical role.

## Do not publish

Do not publish:

* client-confidential data;
* passwords, tokens, credentials or internal infrastructure details;
* unpublished pricing unless management has approved it for public use;
* internal margins, staffing assumptions or sales tactics;
* unsupported outcome claims;
* guarantees of rankings, AI citations, leads or revenue;
* private client names, results or screenshots without approval.

## Service truth

When an internal planning document conflicts with the current approved website/service architecture, resolve the conflict before publication. Do not silently merge contradictory versions.

## Advisory boundaries

AI Consultation and Marketplace & Platform Consultation must remain clearly advisory in public copy unless a separately approved service offer changes that boundary.
