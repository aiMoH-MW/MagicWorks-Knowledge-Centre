# 30/60/90-day tracker

## Baseline

Capture the prior 90 days where available:

* indexed URLs;
* GSC clicks, impressions, CTR and query breadth;
* Bing search data;
* organic landing pages;
* AI/referral sessions where identifiable;
* service-page navigation from knowledge pages;
* enquiries and qualified leads.

## Day 30

Focus on:

* crawl/index status;
* sitemap and canonical behaviour;
* tracking quality;
* early impressions;
* broken links and rendering issues.

## Day 60

Focus on:

* new queries;
* query breadth;
* pages gaining visibility;
* AI/referral changes;
* knowledge-to-service navigation;
* content gaps.

## Day 90

Focus on:

* sustained visibility patterns;
* qualified traffic;
* assisted enquiries/conversions;
* strongest and weakest topic clusters;
* pages to refresh, consolidate or expand.

## Interpretation

Do not claim causation solely because a metric increased after launch. Annotate major site releases, campaigns and external factors.
