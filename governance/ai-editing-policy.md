# AI-assisted editing policy

AI tools may assist with research organisation, outlines, rewriting, QA and first drafts.

They must not become the source of truth.

## Before publishing AI-assisted content

Verify:

* service names and current scope;
* product names and relationships;
* dates and statistics;
* client facts;
* technical claims;
* legal, medical, financial or safety-sensitive statements;
* outbound citations and URLs.

## Prohibited behaviour

Do not use AI to fabricate:

* customer results,
* testimonials,
* case studies,
* research citations,
* awards,
* team credentials,
* partnerships,
* product capabilities.

## Editing principle

AI may improve expression. It may not silently change approved business policy.
