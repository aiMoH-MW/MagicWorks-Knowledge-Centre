# Editorial style guide

## Page design

Each page should have one primary question or concept.

Prefer:

1. a direct answer near the top;
2. short explanatory sections;
3. bullets where they improve scanability;
4. explicit limits and trade-offs;
5. links to the relevant commercial destination where useful.

## Tone

Use clear business language. Avoid exaggerated AI claims, hype, jargon stacking and generic filler.

## Evidence

Distinguish:

* documented MagicWorks practice,
* externally supported facts,
* interpretation,
* recommendations,
* and planning assumptions.

When evidence is required, cite it or remove the claim.

## Search and AI retrieval

Use descriptive headings. Define important concepts explicitly. Avoid creating multiple pages that answer the same question with slightly different wording.

## Dates

Use dates when freshness matters. Do not imply that an old benchmark is current.

## Calls to action

Educational pages may link to a relevant service or contact page, but should not turn every section into a sales pitch.
