# Monthly maintenance SOP

## 1. Review changes in the business

Check whether service scope, product relationships, terminology, leadership, contact paths or commercial URLs changed.

## 2. Review search demand

Use GSC and Bing data to identify:

* emerging queries;
* pages gaining impressions but weak CTR;
* questions with no strong landing page;
* declining pages that may be stale.

## 3. Review AI/referral discovery

Where observable, review AI referral landing pages, source clicks and topic patterns. Do not treat citation-count tools as the sole success metric.

## 4. Review knowledge gaps

Collect unanswered sales questions, support questions, proposal objections and site-search queries.

## 5. Improve before expanding

Refresh weak/high-potential pages before adding large numbers of new pages.

## 6. QA

Run broken-link, indexing and analytics checks.

## 7. Changelog

Record material structural, policy or framework updates.
