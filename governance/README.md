# Governance

Internal maintenance rules for the MagicWorks Knowledge & Resource Centre.

These files are repository-only operating documentation. They are not included in any GitBook `SUMMARY.md` and therefore are not intended as public knowledge pages.

## Core principle

The main MagicWorks website is the canonical commercial surface. The knowledge centre is the explanatory and reference layer.

## Files

* `content-governance.md` — what may and may not be published
* `editorial-style-guide.md` — page-writing conventions
* `canonical-policy.md` — roles of main site vs knowledge centre
* `internal-linking.md` — linking and measurement rules
* `ai-editing-policy.md` — rules for AI-assisted drafting
* `qa-checklist.md` — pre-publication checks
* `monthly-sop.md` — recurring maintenance
* `30-60-90-tracker.md` — launch measurement plan
* `management-summary.md` — management-level operating summary
* `branding.md` — approved visual identity, logo assets and navigation
* `ai-assistant.md` — grounded Assistant prompts and guardrails
* `prelaunch-checklist.md` — final launch readiness checklist
* `gitbook-ui-settings.md` — final GitBook theme, AI, domain, analytics and publication settings
