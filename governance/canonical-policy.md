# Canonical policy

## Main website

`https://magicworksitsolutions.com/` is the canonical commercial surface for:

* service offers,
* commercial positioning,
* contact/enquiry journeys,
* portfolio/work pages,
* current conversion pages.

## Knowledge centre

`https://knowledge.magicworksitsolutions.com/` is the canonical explanatory surface for:

* definitions,
* frameworks,
* FAQs,
* comparisons,
* buyer guides,
* methodology explanations,
* knowledge governance,
* case-study learnings.

## Avoid duplication

Do not copy a full service page into GitBook.

Instead:

* summarise the concept;
* explain decisions and trade-offs;
* link to the commercial page for scope and enquiry;
* keep one clear canonical destination for sales intent.

## Canonical tags

Technical canonical implementation should follow the final deployment architecture. Do not point every knowledge page at a commercial page if the knowledge page has distinct search intent and unique content.
