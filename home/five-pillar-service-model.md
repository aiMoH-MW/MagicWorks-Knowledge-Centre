# The five-pillar service model

MagicWorks organises its work into five pillars:

1. **Digital Marketing**
2. **Web Development**
3. **AI Consultation**
4. **Marketplace & Platform Consultation**
5. **Brand, Research & Publishing**

Three are primarily **delivery pillars**: Digital Marketing, Web Development, and Brand, Research & Publishing.

Two are primarily **advisory pillars**: AI Consultation and Marketplace & Platform Consultation.

The distinction matters. Delivery engagements produce work that MagicWorks executes. Advisory engagements produce judgment, diagnostics, roadmaps and recommendations; implementation may then be handled by the client's team, an appropriate product, MagicWorks under a separately scoped delivery engagement, or another provider chosen by the client.

This separation protects clarity and keeps advisory recommendations independent.
