## Five pillars, one operating model

MagicWorks organises its work around five focused pillars:

1. **Digital Marketing**
2. **Web Development**
3. **AI Consultation**
4. **Marketplace & Platform Consultation**
5. **Brand, Research & Publishing**

Three are primarily **delivery pillars**: Digital Marketing, Web Development, and Brand, Research & Publishing.

Two are primarily **advisory pillars**: AI Consultation and Marketplace & Platform Consultation.

That distinction is intentional. It makes clear what MagicWorks executes directly and where clients are buying independent diagnosis, judgment and a roadmap.

## AI and machine-readable access

The Knowledge Centre is designed to be useful to both people and AI systems.

After publication on the custom domain, GitBook provides machine-readable access through:

* `/llms.txt`
* `/llms-full.txt`
* Markdown versions of published pages
* the read-only published-docs MCP endpoint at `/~gitbook/mcp`

Only intentionally published knowledge belongs in this public layer. Credentials, private governance files, confidential client information and internal operating material remain outside the public boundary.
