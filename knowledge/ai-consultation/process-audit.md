# AI process audit

An AI process audit examines a real business process before choosing technology.

The audit should map the current workflow, inputs, systems, people, handoffs, failure points, data access and measurable outcomes.

It should then identify where AI may help, where deterministic automation is enough, and where human judgment must remain central.

The output is not "use AI." It is a prioritised roadmap with explicit assumptions, risks, build-vs-buy decisions and pilot candidates.
