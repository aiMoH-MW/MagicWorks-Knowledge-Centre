# Build vs buy

The right build-vs-buy decision starts with the workflow, not the model.

## Consider buying when

A mature product already solves most of the workflow, the process is relatively standard, speed matters, and differentiation is low.

## Consider building when

The workflow is strategically distinctive, integration depth is high, proprietary data matters, or existing products create unacceptable constraints.

## Consider a hybrid approach when

A standard platform can handle commodity parts of the workflow while custom integration, retrieval, governance or user experience creates the real advantage.

The decision should also account for operating cost, maintenance, security, model changes, vendor lock-in and the internal team's ability to own the system after launch.
