# AI vendor evaluation

AI vendor evaluation should test the product against the client's workflow, not against a polished demo.

Useful criteria include:

* fit to the target process,
* integration requirements,
* data handling and retention,
* security and access controls,
* evaluation methods,
* model and infrastructure dependencies,
* human-review options,
* portability and exit risk,
* operating cost,
* and the vendor's ability to support the implementation.

A proof of concept should use representative tasks and known edge cases. A vendor that performs well on a generic demo may still be a poor fit for the real process.
