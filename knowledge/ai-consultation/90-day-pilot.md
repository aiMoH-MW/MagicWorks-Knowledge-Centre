# The 90-day AI pilot

A good AI pilot tests a business hypothesis, not just a technology demo.

## Days 0–30: define and baseline

Document the current workflow, establish baseline metrics, identify risks and define the human-review model.

## Days 31–60: implement and evaluate

Run the solution with a controlled user group. Measure quality, speed, error modes, adoption and operating cost.

## Days 61–90: validate the operating model

Decide whether the result is reliable enough to scale. Document exceptions, ownership, governance, training needs and integration requirements.

At the end of the pilot, the decision may be **scale, refine or stop**. Stopping a weak use case is a successful outcome when it prevents a larger bad investment.
