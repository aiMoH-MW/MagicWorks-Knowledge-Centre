# AI readiness

AI readiness is not the same as having access to an AI model.

A business is more ready when it has:

* a clear problem worth solving,
* a repeatable process,
* usable data and system access,
* an accountable process owner,
* measurable success criteria,
* appropriate human oversight,
* and governance proportional to the risk.

The best early use cases are usually repetitive, measurable and operationally important. A process that is unstable, undocumented or politically ownerless is a poor automation candidate even if the underlying AI technology is capable.
