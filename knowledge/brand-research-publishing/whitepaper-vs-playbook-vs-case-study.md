# Whitepaper vs playbook vs case study

Choose the format based on the job the asset needs to do.

## Whitepaper

Use a whitepaper to develop a researched position, explain a problem space or synthesise evidence for a decision-maker.

## Playbook

Use a playbook to codify a repeatable method, framework or operating procedure.

## Case study

Use a case study to document an actual situation, intervention and outcome, with evidence and limitations.

The strongest content programmes often use all three, but they should not be treated as interchangeable labels for long PDFs.
