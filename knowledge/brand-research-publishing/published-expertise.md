# Whitepapers, playbooks and case studies

Different publishing formats solve different problems.

**Whitepapers** develop a researched position on a defined topic.

**Playbooks** explain a repeatable method or operating model.

**Case studies** document what was actually done, what changed and what evidence supports the result.

All three can strengthen authority when they are specific, evidence-led and clearly attributed. They become especially valuable when they are linked to service pages, sales conversations, knowledge pages and follow-on content rather than published as isolated PDFs.
