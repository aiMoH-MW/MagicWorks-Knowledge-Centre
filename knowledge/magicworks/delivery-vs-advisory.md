# Delivery vs advisory

MagicWorks separates execution from independent advice.

## Delivery pillars

Digital Marketing, Web Development, and Brand, Research & Publishing are delivery-oriented. The client buys defined work and MagicWorks is responsible for producing it.

## Advisory pillars

AI Consultation and Marketplace & Platform Consultation are advisory-oriented. The client buys diagnosis, judgment, prioritisation and a roadmap.

If an advisory engagement identifies an implementation need, the recommendation should remain transparent. Execution can be handled by the client's team, a suitable SaaS product, MagicWorks under a separate scope, or an external vendor.

This distinction keeps the advisor's role clear and helps clients evaluate implementation options on their merits.
