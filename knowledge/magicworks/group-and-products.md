# MagicWorks group and products

MagicWorks IT Solutions operates alongside productised offerings in the broader MagicWorks group.

The service architecture distinguishes consulting and delivery work from productised software so clients can see what they are buying.

Where an advisory engagement identifies a problem already solved well by a suitable product, that product can be introduced separately. Where a client needs independent implementation or another vendor, the advisory recommendation should remain free to say so.

This separation helps prevent a consultation from becoming a disguised product pitch.
