# Service architecture

MagicWorks uses a focused five-pillar architecture so each service has a clear buying motion, delivery model and accountability boundary.

The five pillars are Digital Marketing, Web Development, AI Consultation, Marketplace & Platform Consultation, and Brand, Research & Publishing.

A focused architecture is useful for clients because it makes three things explicit:

* what MagicWorks will execute,
* what MagicWorks will advise on,
* and what should be handled by a product, a client team or another specialist.

This structure also reduces the risk of presenting every capability as a standalone service simply because the company can technically perform it.
