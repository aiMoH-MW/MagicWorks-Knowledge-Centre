# SEO, AEO, GEO & AI Search

Search discovery now spans traditional search results, answer surfaces, AI-generated summaries and conversational assistants.

The useful response is not to replace SEO with a new acronym. It is to make the organisation's expertise easier to crawl, understand, retrieve, trust and connect to business outcomes.

MagicWorks treats SEO, AEO and GEO as related disciplines with different emphasis:

* **SEO** improves discoverability and performance in search engines.
* **AEO** structures information so direct answers can be extracted reliably.
* **GEO** strengthens the quality, authority and retrievability of content for generative answer experiences.
