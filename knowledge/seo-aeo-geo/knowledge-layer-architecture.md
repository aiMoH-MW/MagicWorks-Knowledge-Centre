# Knowledge-layer architecture

A knowledge layer is a structured public reference that explains an organisation's expertise, concepts, methods and decision guidance.

It should complement the main website rather than duplicate it.

The commercial site remains the canonical place for service offers, product pages and conversion journeys. The knowledge layer goes deeper on definitions, frameworks, comparisons, FAQs, evidence and buyer questions.

A strong architecture usually includes:

* a clear taxonomy,
* stable canonical topics,
* concise answer pages,
* trust and governance pages,
* deliberate links back to commercial destinations,
* and measurement that distinguishes knowledge discovery from commercial conversion.
