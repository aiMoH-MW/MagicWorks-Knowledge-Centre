# Trust and evidence

Strong retrieval is not only about technical accessibility. Sources also need to make their credibility legible.

Useful trust signals include:

* named authors or accountable organisations,
* clear editorial ownership,
* references where factual claims depend on external evidence,
* publication and update dates where relevant,
* correction mechanisms,
* transparent claims boundaries,
* and consistency between public pages.

For regulated, financial, medical, legal or safety-sensitive topics, governance should be stronger still.

Trust signals do not guarantee ranking or citation, but they reduce ambiguity about who is making the claim and why the source should be taken seriously.
