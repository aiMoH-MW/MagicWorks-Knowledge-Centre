# SEO vs AEO vs GEO

SEO, AEO and GEO overlap, but they are not identical.

## SEO

SEO focuses on crawlability, indexing, relevance, authority, user experience and search performance.

## AEO

Answer Engine Optimisation focuses on making important questions and answers explicit. Definitions, FAQs, comparisons, structured headings and semantic clarity all help.

## GEO

Generative Engine Optimisation focuses on whether high-quality sources are understandable and useful to generative systems. It depends heavily on expertise, evidence, entity clarity, distinctiveness and consistent publication.

No agency controls whether a specific AI system will cite a particular page. The responsible promise is to improve the conditions for discovery and retrieval, then measure what changes.
