# AI Search Visibility

AI Search Visibility is the ability of a business, expert, product or source to be correctly understood and surfaced across AI-influenced discovery experiences.

A strong foundation usually includes:

* clear entities and subject authority,
* answer-first pages for real questions,
* crawlable and indexable technical foundations,
* evidence and visible authorship,
* strong internal linking,
* useful comparisons and decision guides,
* and measurement across search, referrals and commercial behaviour.

A knowledge layer helps because it gives important questions a stable, structured home rather than scattering answers across unrelated long-form pages.

Visibility should be treated as a measurable improvement programme, not a promise of guaranteed citations.
