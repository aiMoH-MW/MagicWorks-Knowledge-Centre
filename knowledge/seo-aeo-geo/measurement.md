# How to measure a knowledge layer

Measurement should move from technical visibility to commercial contribution.

## Technical health

Track indexing, crawl issues, canonicals, sitemaps and structured-data validity.

## Search discovery

Use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to monitor clicks, impressions, query breadth and landing-page performance.

## AI and referral behaviour

Use GA4 to identify referral sessions from AI and other discovery surfaces where referrer data is available.

## Knowledge contribution

Track entrances to knowledge pages, onward clicks to services or products, downloads, enquiry actions and other meaningful events.

## Business outcomes

Where CRM or lead data is available, evaluate qualified enquiries, assisted conversions and pipeline influence.

For an owned knowledge subdomain and main website that belong to one journey, prefer appropriate GA4 domain/subdomain measurement and event design rather than adding internal UTM parameters to every owned navigation link.
