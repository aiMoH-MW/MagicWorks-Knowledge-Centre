# The ANSWER Framework

The **ANSWER Framework** is MagicWorks' method for turning expertise into a structured, measurable knowledge system.

## A — Authority & entity definition

Clarify the organisation, experts, services, products, programmes, locations and areas of subject authority.

## N — Need & query discovery

Map the questions, comparisons, objections and decision intents that real prospects have.

## S — Structured knowledge

Build the definitions, evidence, FAQs, glossaries, comparisons, guides and decision architecture required to answer those needs.

## W — Web & AI accessibility

Make the knowledge crawlable, linked, indexable and machine-readable through strong technical foundations.

## E — Evidence & expertise

Make authorship, citations, review standards, claims boundaries and corrections visible.

## R — Retrieval measurement

Measure search visibility, AI visibility where observable, referrals, engagement, conversions and knowledge gaps.

The framework is iterative: discover, structure, publish, measure, identify gaps, then improve selectively.
