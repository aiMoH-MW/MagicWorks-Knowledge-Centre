# Marketplace & Platform Consultation

Marketplace and platform businesses face decisions that ordinary websites do not: cold starts, two-sided liquidity, trust, pricing, seller quality, matching, transactions, governance and platform economics.

MagicWorks treats this as an advisory discipline. The value is senior judgment and a sequenced roadmap, not an obligation to sell the client a build.

A strong platform strategy connects business model, demand and supply, trust, product architecture, go-to-market, risk and defensibility.
