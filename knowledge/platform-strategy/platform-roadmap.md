# Platform roadmap decisions

A useful platform roadmap should make the difficult choices visible.

Typical decision areas include:

* business model and monetisation,
* target market and wedge,
* supply acquisition,
* demand acquisition,
* cold-start strategy,
* trust and quality controls,
* payments and transactions,
* search and matching,
* product architecture,
* AI opportunities,
* operating model,
* and defensibility.

The roadmap should sequence these choices. Trying to solve every side of a marketplace simultaneously often spreads capital and management attention too thin.
