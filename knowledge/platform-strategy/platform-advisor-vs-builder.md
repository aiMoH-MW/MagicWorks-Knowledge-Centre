# Platform advisor vs builder

A builder is accountable for implementation. An advisor is accountable for judgment.

Platform founders often need both, but the roles should not be confused.

An independent advisor can help evaluate business model choices, marketplace liquidity, architecture, vendors, AI opportunities, growth priorities and sequencing without every recommendation being shaped by what the advisor's delivery team wants to sell.

If implementation is required, it can be scoped separately. Keeping the advisory decision distinct from the build decision makes trade-offs easier to examine.
