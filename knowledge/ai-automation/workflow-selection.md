# Selecting workflows for automation

Before choosing a model or platform, score the workflow itself.

Useful questions include:

* How frequently does the task occur?
* Is the input digital and accessible?
* Can output quality be evaluated?
* What happens when the system is wrong?
* Are exceptions common or rare?
* Is there a clear human owner?
* Can the process be measured before and after automation?

High-volume but low-consequence workflows can be good early candidates. High-risk workflows may still benefit from AI, but usually require tighter controls, narrower scope and stronger human review.
