# Human oversight

Human oversight should be designed into the workflow rather than added after something goes wrong.

The right level depends on consequence. A low-risk drafting assistant may need periodic sampling. A workflow affecting money, legal obligations, employment, health, safety or customer commitments may require review before action.

Useful controls include approval gates, confidence thresholds, exception queues, source citations, audit logs, rollback paths and clear escalation ownership.

The goal is not to keep a human clicking every button forever. It is to place human judgment where errors would matter most.
