# Measurement and attribution

A useful measurement system separates several layers:

* technical tracking quality,
* traffic and acquisition,
* engagement,
* conversion events,
* lead quality,
* opportunity progression,
* and revenue contribution where data allows.

GA4 is useful for behavioural measurement, but it should not be treated as the only source of truth. Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, ad platforms, CRM or lead systems, and server-side/business data may each answer different parts of the question.

Attribution is never perfect. The goal is to make decisions with better evidence, not to pretend every customer journey can be reduced to one last-click number.
