# Digital Marketing

Digital marketing should connect traffic and attention to measurable business outcomes.

MagicWorks treats search, paid media, content, email, social, conversion and measurement as parts of a connected growth system rather than isolated channel activities.

A useful digital marketing programme answers four questions:

1. Where does qualified demand come from?
2. What happens after the click?
3. Can attribution distinguish useful activity from vanity metrics?
4. What should be changed next based on evidence?
