# Full-funnel digital marketing

A full-funnel programme connects channels instead of optimising each one in isolation.

Paid search can create demand capture. SEO and answer-oriented content can expand discovery. Social and thought leadership can create familiarity. Email can nurture known audiences. Conversion work determines whether that attention becomes useful action.

The operating question is not "which channel wins?" It is "how do the channels, landing experiences and measurement system work together to create qualified demand?"

A full-funnel approach should still preserve channel accountability. Bundling is not an excuse to hide weak performance.
