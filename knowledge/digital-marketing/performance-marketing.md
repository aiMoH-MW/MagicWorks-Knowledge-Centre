# Performance marketing

Performance marketing is strongest when the measurement system is designed before campaign optimisation begins.

That means defining meaningful conversions, checking tracking quality, understanding lead quality and connecting channel activity to downstream outcomes where possible.

A campaign can generate cheap leads and still underperform commercially. Conversely, a channel with higher acquisition cost can be valuable if it generates stronger sales-qualified opportunities.

The practical objective is not the lowest headline cost. It is **accountable growth** with enough instrumentation to understand what is actually creating value.
