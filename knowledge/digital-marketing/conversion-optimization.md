# Conversion optimisation

Traffic acquisition cannot compensate indefinitely for a weak destination.

Conversion optimisation looks at the path from visit to meaningful action: page speed, message clarity, mobile usability, form friction, trust signals, landing-page relevance and measurement quality.

The goal is not to maximise every click-through rate. It is to reduce avoidable friction for the right visitors while preserving lead quality.

Before changing design, establish a baseline. Without before-and-after measurement, "conversion optimisation" can become a sequence of subjective redesigns.
