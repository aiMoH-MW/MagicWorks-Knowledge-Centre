# Choosing a digital marketing partner

Ask how the partner connects channel activity to business outcomes.

Useful evaluation questions include:

* What data is required before work starts?
* How are conversions and lead quality defined?
* Who owns analytics and tracking quality?
* How are SEO, paid media, content and conversion issues separated?
* What happens when the evidence says a channel should be reduced?
* Can the team explain what it will not do?

A strong partner should be able to distinguish activity from progress and should be comfortable discussing attribution limitations rather than hiding behind dashboards.
