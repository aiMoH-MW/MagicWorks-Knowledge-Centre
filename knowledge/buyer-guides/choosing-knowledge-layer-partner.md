# Choosing a knowledge-layer partner

A knowledge-layer partner should understand information architecture, SEO/AEO, web implementation, editorial governance and measurement.

Ask whether the proposed solution:

* keeps content portable,
* distinguishes the knowledge layer from the main commercial site,
* has a canonical and internal-linking strategy,
* includes trust and evidence standards,
* measures indexing, search visibility, referrals and conversions,
* avoids guarantees of AI citations,
* and can be maintained after the initial build.

The platform matters less than the operating model. A good partner should be able to explain how the content remains useful even if the underlying documentation technology changes.
