# Choosing a web development partner

Evaluate more than visual design.

A modern web partner should be able to explain:

* architecture and content management,
* performance and mobile experience,
* accessibility,
* analytics and conversion tracking,
* SEO and semantic structure,
* deployment and maintenance,
* security responsibilities,
* and how future AI features would be added without unnecessary replatforming.

The right stack depends on the business. The important signal is whether the partner can explain the trade-offs rather than prescribing one technology for every brief.
