# Choosing an AI consultant

A useful AI consultant should begin with processes and decisions, not with a preferred model or vendor.

Look for evidence that the advisor can:

* identify workflows worth analysing,
* explain where AI should not be used,
* compare build and buy options,
* define measurable pilots,
* address governance and human oversight,
* and separate advisory recommendations from implementation incentives.

Be cautious when every diagnostic conveniently ends with the consultant selling the same platform or custom build.
