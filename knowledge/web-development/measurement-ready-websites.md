# Measurement-ready websites

Measurement should be part of the website architecture, not an afterthought added at launch.

A measurement-ready site has clearly defined key events, consistent data-layer conventions where needed, form-success tracking, consent-aware analytics, stable page identities and a plan for connecting website actions with lead or CRM outcomes.

This matters because redesigns often destroy historical continuity or create duplicate and unreliable events.

Good implementation makes future marketing analysis easier and reduces the cost of diagnosing conversion problems.
