# Technical foundations for search and AI

Search and AI systems both benefit from websites that are structurally clear.

Important foundations include:

* semantic HTML,
* stable URLs,
* clean internal linking,
* canonical consistency,
* XML sitemaps,
* sensible robots controls,
* structured data where it adds clarity,
* fast mobile performance,
* accessible content,
* and clear separation between primary content and decorative interface elements.

Technical work cannot manufacture authority, but weak technical foundations can prevent strong content from being discovered or interpreted correctly.
