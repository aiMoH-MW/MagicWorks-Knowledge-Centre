# Next.js vs WordPress

Neither Next.js nor WordPress is automatically the right choice.

## Next.js is often a better fit when

The site needs custom application behaviour, strong performance control, headless content, deep integrations, AI-assisted features or a roadmap that is likely to become more product-like over time.

## WordPress is often a better fit when

The requirement is a straightforward content-led site, the internal team strongly prefers the WordPress editorial model, the integration needs are modest, and the additional engineering flexibility of a custom stack would not create enough business value.

The decision should be based on roadmap, governance, budget, internal capability and maintenance—not fashion.
