# Case-study learnings

Case studies are most useful when they do more than celebrate a project.

A strong case-study learning page separates:

* the original business problem,
* the constraints,
* the intervention,
* the evidence,
* what can reasonably be attributed to the work,
* what remains uncertain,
* and the lesson that can transfer to other situations.

This section will grow as MagicWorks publishes client-approved case studies and internal proof assets.
