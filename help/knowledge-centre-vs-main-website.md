# Knowledge centre vs main website

The main MagicWorks website is the canonical commercial surface for service descriptions, enquiries and conversion journeys.

The knowledge centre is the explanatory layer. It can go deeper on definitions, frameworks, trade-offs, FAQs and decision guidance without turning every educational page into a sales page.

Where the two overlap, the knowledge page should support the commercial page rather than duplicate it.
