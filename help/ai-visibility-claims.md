# AI visibility claims

MagicWorks does not guarantee placement, citation or inclusion in ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews or any other AI answer system.

The responsible objective is to improve the conditions that support discovery: clear entities, useful answers, evidence, crawlability, authority, structured knowledge and measurement.

Reported outcomes should distinguish visibility, referral traffic, engagement and commercial contribution.
